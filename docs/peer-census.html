How to read it

Usable means the handshake completed and the node advertises NODE_COMPACT_FILTERS; Winnow refuses anything else at the door. At the tip means within a hundred blocks of the 95th-percentile reported height, the same tolerance the wallet's pool applies before unseating a peer. Stuck at the split means reporting a height within twenty blocks of 961,632, where the BIP-110 minority chain left the main chain on 2026-08-08 and stopped.

The census judges what a node reports, never which software it runs. The family column is there because the answer turned out to depend on it. Onion and I2P addresses are not dialled. Per-node detail is kept as a two-week workflow artifact; this page carries aggregates only. Source: WinnowCensus in the repository, run by the Peer census workflow.
