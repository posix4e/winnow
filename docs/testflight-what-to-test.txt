Peers and sync

Let the wallet sync from a fresh install, or use Sync now in Settings, and open Connected peers while it runs. No peer should keep its seat while reporting a tip thousands of blocks behind the others; such a peer is dropped and its slot refilled. The filter scan should reach the current tip, with no sync error line.

Background: about one in twelve compact-filter peers on the network is stuck on the BIP-110 fork chain from August. The previous build could stall on one of them. This build unseats them, fails over when one hangs up mid-sync, and ships a fallback peer list with none on it.

Sending

Send a small amount on the network you use. It should be announced only to peers whose fee filter accepts it, and confirm as before.
