People and shared savings

Open People and tap Share my card: give it a name and send the card to someone (or to your other device). On the other side, Add person and paste it: it should read "Fresh address each payment" and "Can co-own savings". Pay that person from their screen twice; the review names them, shows the address underneath, and the second payment goes to a different address.

Create shared savings with one or two people: the threshold defaults to 2, and creating ends on the savings card. Share that card and add it on the other phone before sending any money in. Then send a small amount to the savings' address, wait for a block, and use Ask for approval on one phone and Approve a request on the other; when enough have approved, Finish sends it.

Beginner mode

The wallet starts with four tabs and a one-line sync status. Settings shows Backup, the Advanced mode switch, About and Delete wallet only. Turn Advanced mode on: the test network, peers, chain verification, the block explorer, build details and a Vaults section on the People tab appear. Turn it off again: they go, and anything you set stays.
